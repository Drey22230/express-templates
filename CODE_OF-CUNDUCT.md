# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who contributes to or uses this project.

All participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate of others.
* Providing constructive feedback.
* Being open to different opinions and approaches.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
* Respecting other contributors regardless of their experience level.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Insulting or threatening other contributors.
* Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or open-source community.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a GitHub issue or contacting the project maintainer privately when appropriate.

Reports will be handled respectfully and reviewed as appropriate.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to unacceptable behavior, including removing inappropriate content or restricting participation in the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related spaces.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.
